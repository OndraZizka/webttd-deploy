If you know OpenTTD, roads, stations, vehicles, cargo, and routes will feel familiar. WebTTD is not a one-player
replacement for OpenTTD: it is designed for a shared multiplayer economy where several players can build in the same world.

## A slower, longer-running game

WebTTD is intended for players who may check in for a while, plan a connection, and let it earn money before building the
next one. The pace is deliberately slower: expect to make a few useful connections over time rather than hundreds in a
single day.

Compared with OpenTTD, the current MMO design uses:

- slower vehicle movement and industry production;
- higher construction and vehicle costs;
- more space between industries, leaving room for many players to build;
- incentives to reuse existing roads, railways, and stations where access is available.

These choices are meant to support a world that players can share and revisit casually but regularly. They also mean that
a successful OpenTTD strategy may need a different budget or timetable here. Check live prices and service results in the
game rather than assuming the two economies are identical.

## Build alongside other players

Players can build independently or share a company. Existing infrastructure can be valuable to a new route, but it is not
automatically available in every case; check ownership, access, and any rental terms. Use company chat to coordinate shared
projects and avoid surprising teammates with major construction or spending.

> WebTTD is still under development. Features, balance, and world data may change, and worlds may be reset.
