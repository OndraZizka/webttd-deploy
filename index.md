WebTTD is a multiplayer transport and company-building game inspired by OpenTTD. The map is shared: players can build
their own networks, work together in a company, and make use of infrastructure created by other players when access is
available.

Your first goal is simple: find a need, connect it with a road or railway, and run a vehicle that can serve it. Towns
create passengers; industries produce goods. Deliveries bring in revenue that you can put toward your next useful link.

## Your first few minutes

1. Sign in from the **Game, company login and server** toolbar menu. If you do not have a company yet, choose **Start a
   company** or **Join an existing company**.
2. Pan the map by right-dragging and zoom with the mouse wheel. The **Town directory** and **Industry directory** can help
   you find a starting area.
3. Pick one short connection. Look for an existing road or track you can reuse before paying to build a new one.
4. Build matching stops, a depot, and a vehicle; then create and assign a route. The in-game **Help** window also gives a
   quick construction checklist.

> **Development notice:** WebTTD is in active development. Game rules, prices, and features can change, and world data may
> be reset while the game is being developed.

## A good network grows in stages

Start with a service that is easy to understand and maintain. Check that the stops can collect the cargo you want to carry,
that the destination accepts it, and that your company can afford both construction and vehicle running costs. When the
service is working, use the company and vehicle finance windows to decide what to improve next.
